HOW DOES A STACK WORK?

A stack is a linear data structure where the last element added is the first element removed. Imagine a pile of plates: you place a plate on top and take the top plate first.

Four Main Operations

  • Push: Add a new element to the top of the stack.
  • Pop: Remove the element at the top of the stack.
  • Peek: View the top element without removing it.
  • Display: View all elements from top to bottom.

STEP-BY-STEP EXAMPLE

Suppose we perform these operations in order:

  1. Push 10 — the stack contains 10.
  2. Push 20 — 20 becomes the new top.
  3. Push 30 — 30 becomes the new top.
  4. Peek — returns 30 without changing the stack.
  5. Pop — removes 30, so 20 becomes the new top.
